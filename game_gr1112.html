The Year Is 2047

The world's most important library — The Archive of Everything — has been swallowed by a mysterious fog. Every book, every formula, every map, every story ever written is locked inside.

A team of young explorers has been chosen. Not for their speed. Not for their strength. But for their minds.

You are one of them.

The fog has four gates. Each gate is sealed by a different kind of challenge — a puzzle, a question of reason, a test of memory, a word only the wise can unlock.

But here is what the fog does: it feeds on mistakes. Every wrong answer pushes you deeper into it. Every hasty guess costs you ground.

"Move too fast and the fog wins. Steady the pace — and the Archive is yours."
